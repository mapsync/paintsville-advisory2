FEBRUARY 4, 2021

We have experienced a main line break at the intersection of Euclid Avenue and Frank Street. The water is quickly freezing, resulting in hazardous driving conditions. Please avoid this area this morning if possible. Our crew is on the scene to make the repair. The west bound lane of Euclid will be closed until repairs are completed.

Water service is interrupted from Stafford Street to Preston Street.

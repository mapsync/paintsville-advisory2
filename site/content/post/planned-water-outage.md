In order to complete the final steps of a project designed to strengthen water supply to Flat Gap and part of the Staffordsville and Oil Springs service areas, service will be interrupted to part of the RT 40 West area **Monday, March 7, 2022.**  Weather permitting, the service interruption will begin about 9:00am and we expect service to be restored by 3:00 pm.  The affected areas are:

**RT 40 W from Old Bridge Road, but not including Barnetts Creek**

**Trimble Lane**

**Rt 2275**

**Fishtrap Road**

**Blanton Subdivision**

**Paintsville Lake State Park**

When service is restored, the affected areas will be placed under a precautionary boil water advisory.

BOIL WATER ADVISORY

December 3, 2024

Due to a line break, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for the following area: 

All of Woodland Estates in Paintsville

[](<>)This is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice. Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the effected areas to boil their water before using it, for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth, or food preparation. Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes. Then let it cool before using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

On Friday afternoon, Paintsville Utilities performed the necessary replacement of a residential gas meter in Cross Creek. To make this repair, the gas had to be turned off at that residence. In accordance with Public Service Commission regulations, Paintsville Utilities’ policy is to not turn gas meters on when no one is home. Unfortunately, our technician did not make proper contact with the customer before making the repair. When management learned of this error, a[](<>)technician was dispatched, and the customer’s service was restored Friday afternoon. This regrettable error was an isolated incident. Paintsville Utilities is NOT shutting off gas meters in Cross Creek.

BOIL WATER ADVISORY



September 20, 2020



Due to a line break, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for:

1. RT 321 N, including all side roads, beginning at the RT 40 intersection to and including Stewart Road,

2. RT 460 W, including all side roads, beginning at the intersection with RT 321 continuing to and including Horne Branch and Verne Horne Drive.

Hidden Valley is included.

3. RT 2039, including all side roads, from RT 460 to but not including ASA Creek.

4. RT 40 W, including all side roads, beginning at RT 321 continuing to but not including Barnett’s Creek. Cross Creek is included.

5. RT 172, including all side roads, from RT 40 to Ramey Branch Church. Stone Coal is NOT included.

6. US 23, including all side roads, from the intersection with RT 460 to the intersection with RT 825.

This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice.

Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the affected areas to boil their water before using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth or food preparation. Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

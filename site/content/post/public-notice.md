FINAL PUBLIC NOTICE Paintsville Utilities applied for Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Public Assistance (PA) Program funding through Kentucky Emergency Management (KYEM) as a sub- recipient.
Under the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA), federal actions must be reviewed and evaluated for feasible alternatives and for social, economic, historic, environmental, legal, and safety considerations. Under Executive Order (EO) 11988 and EO 11990, FEMA is required to consider alternatives, and to provide a public notice of any proposed actions in or affecting floodplains or wetlands. EO 12898 also requires FEMA to provide the opportunity for public participation in the planning process and to consider potential impacts to minority and low-income populations. This notice may also fulfill requirements under Section 106 of the National Historic Preservation Act (NHPA).
Funding for the proposed project will be conditional upon compliance with all applicable federal, tribal, state, and local laws, regulations, floodplain standards, permit requirements and conditions.

Special Flood Hazard Area Zone:
This project is for repairs of a utility pipe back to pre-disaster condition. The projects occupancy within the floodplain was determined by referencing FIRM Panel #21115C0191E dated 09/16/2015. The proposed work conforms to all applicable the Commonwealth of Kentucky and local floodplain regulations. There is a potential for the facility to be impacted by future flooding events due to its location within the floodway AE zone. The proposed work will also take place in and adjacent to the Levisa Fork River, a designated riverine habitat per the United States Fish and Wildlife Service National Wetlands Inventory.
Proposed Work and Purpose
SOW for floodplain sites: Per Public Assistance, Damage Inventory (DI) #467039 is ineligible. EHP review has not been completed. If the damages become eligible or there is a change to the scope of work, EHP review must be completed.
For DI #553045, Sub-applicant: Paintsville Utilities, Johnson County Category F– Utilities. Levisa Fork
River Road/CSX Water Crossing (37.802560, -82.791819; 37.801933, -82.791047 corrected
coordinates). Contract and/or force account services will be used to restore the damaged embankment
and pipe back to pre-disaster function through directional boring. Work is 0% complete, $418,087.16.
Hazard mitigation work includes the installation of vegetation cover, live stakes, and coir mats.
Purpose: The purpose of this proposed work is to restore the embankment and utility pipe back to a
usable condition. This will be achieved through best construction practices of directional boring.
Project Alternatives:
Alternative #1 (No Action):
The “no action” alternative would be to leave the roadway and pipe and earthen material as is. This
would be hazardous to those individuals and public services dependent upon these facilities. No action
would likely result in abandonment of this facility and would eliminate an important component within
the community, which would likely lead to adverse impacts.
Alternative #2 (Repair in-kind):
In-kind repair likely won’t cause any adverse effects. Since the embankments and piping have existed
in this location for over 35 years, providing important services for the community.

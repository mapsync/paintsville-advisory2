BOIL WATER ADVISORY

April 13, 2021



Due to a main line break, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for:



US 460 from the intersection with RT 321 to, and including, Verne Horne Drive including all side roads.

RT 40 West from the RT 460 intersection to, but not including, Barnett’s Creek. All side roads are included. This does include all of Cross Creek

RT 172 from the intersection with RT 40 to Ramey Branch Church, including all side roads except Stone Coal, which is not included.

All of RT 2039 including all side roads.

RT 825 from the intersection with RT 1428 to, but not including, Asa Creek.

US 23 from the intersection with RT 460 to the intersection with RT 1428 including all side roads.

RT 1428 from US 23 to RT 302, including all side roads.

All of West Van Lear

All of Van Lear including Powell Addition.

RT 321 from the south side Starfire Hill to, and across, the American Standard bridge to the end of the road, including all side roads.





This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice.

Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the affected areas to boil their water before using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth or food preparation. Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

BOIL WATER ADVISORY



Due to a main line break, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for:



RT 321N from the intersection with RT 40 to RT 23, including all side roads.

US 23 N from the intersection with RT 321 to Lindy Branch, including all side roads.

RT 201 from US 23 to Little Mud Lick Road including all side roads.

RT 993 including all side roads.

RT 581 from the top of Tutor Key hill to the bridge at River including all side roads.

RT 460 from the intersection with RT 321 to and including Verne Horne Drive and all side roads in between,

RT 2039 including all side roads.

RT 825 from RT 2039 to Asa Creek, including all side roads.

RT 40 West from RT 321 to but not including Barnetts Creek and all side roads in between. This does include Cross Creek.

Little Mud Lick from RT 40 to but not including Rule Branch. All side roads are included.

RT 172 from RT 40 to but not including Stone Coal .All side roads in between are included.

Pricetown, Banjo Branch, Chestnut, Whitehouse and Hammond.

This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice.  Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the affected areas to boil their water before using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth or food preparation.  Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using.  Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

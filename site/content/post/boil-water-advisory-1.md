Due to a main line break, although no contamintion has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has

Voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for the following areas:







ALL OF PERRY VANHOOSE MEMORIAL HOUSING COMPLEX ONLY ON WILLIAMS BRANCH IN WITTENSVILLE







This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice, Paintsville Utilities

Is advising all customers in the effected areas to boil water using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes,

Brushing teeth, or food prepartion. Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, then let it cool prior

To using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

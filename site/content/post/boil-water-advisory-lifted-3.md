Paintsville Utilities

Boil Water Advisory Lifted

The boil water advisory for the following areas has been lifted. Starting at the intersection of Puncheon Creek and Rt. 1092 all the way to Hall Branch on Rt. 689. This advisory includes, Hall Branch, Sparks Branch, Franks Creek, Slone Branch, Elwood Lane, Bailey Road, Martin-Preston Road, Flatgap Drive, and Conley Creek to the top of the hill.

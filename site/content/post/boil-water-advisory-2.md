PAINTSVILLE UTILITIES

BOIL WATER ADVISORY

Due to main line maintenance , although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville

Utilities is voluntarily issuing a boil water advisory for the following areas;





Starting at Highland Elementary on US Highway 23 South to the 23 and 825 intersection and

From 825 and 1428 intersection on 825 to 825 and 2039 intersection including all hollows and side

Roads in between, on Middle Fork from US 23 and Coleman Branch but not including Coleman Branch.



This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice.

Paintsville Utilities advises all customers in the effected areas to boil their water before using

It for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth, or any food preparation.

Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using. Boiling

Kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

Due to a line maintenance, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities

Has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for the following areas:





ALL OF MAIN STREET STARTING AT COLLEGE STREET AND MAIN TO END AT THIRD AND MAIN









This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice.

Paintsville Utilities advises all customers in the effect area to boil their water before using it for,

Drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth, or food preparation. Bring

All water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using. Boiling

Kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

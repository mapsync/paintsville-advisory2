Due to a power failure and the resulting pressure drop, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for RT 201 from Little Mud Lick Road to the end of the line in Blaine, including all hollows and side roads in between. Other areas included are: Little Mud Lick Road from RT 201 to and including Rule Branch, including all hollows and side roads. All our customers along RT 32 including all hollows and side roads. RT 469 from RT 32 to the Johnson/Lawrence County line including all hollows and side roads. RT 1559 including all hollows and side roads. RT 3387 including all hollows and side roads. This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice. Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the affected areas to boil their water before using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth, or food preparation. Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

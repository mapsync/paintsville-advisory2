Due to a main line break although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has issued a voluntary boil water advisory for the following

Areas .

From Sparks Branch on 1092 to Halls Branch on 689 This includes all of Halls Branch, Franks Creek, and Conley Branch.



This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice. Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the

Effected areas to boil their water before using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth, or food preparations.

Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, then let it cool before using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

Boil Water Advisory LIFTED\

February 15-2025\
The Boil Water Advisory has been lifted for the following areas:\

- From the intersection of US 460 with KY RT 40 West to Conley Tire on KY RT 40 West.\
- From the intersection of KY RT 40 West with Little Mud Lick Road to the intersection of Rule Branch with Little Mud Lick Road.\
- All of Cross Creek #1 and #2\
This will also include all hollows and side roads in between.

**PAINTSVILLE UTILITIES PLANNED WATER OUTAGE**

Paintsville Utilities will have the water off October 19, 2021 starting around 9:00 a.m. for approximately 4 hours in the following areas:

All of KY RT 993 including RT 581 from Whippoorwill Rd to the County line including Offutt, Whitehouse, and Hammond area.

This outage is due to line maintenance. We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause.

BOIL WATER ADVISORY



12/21/2021



Due to a water line break, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for:



\- RT 40 West beginning at, and including Cross Creek, continuing to, but not including      Barnett’s Creek, including all hollows and side roads in between except RT 580 which is not included.

\- RT 172 from the intersection with RT 40 to Ramey Branch, including all hollows and side roads in between, except Stone Coal which is not included.



This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice.

Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the affected areas to boil their water before using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth or food preparation. Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

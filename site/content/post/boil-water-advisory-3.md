BOIL WATER ADVISORY

July 5, 2025



Due to a line break, although no contamination has been confirmed, Paintsville Utilities has voluntarily issued a boil water advisory for the Williamsport and Boonscamp area as follows:



\- RT 40 E from the bottom of Two Mile Hill to the top of Spicy Mountain. All hollows and side roads in between are included.

\- RT 2040, including all side roads, from RT 40 to the bottom of Offutt Hill.

\- RT 1596, including all side roads, from RT 40 to the end of the line.



This advisory is being issued as a precautionary measure and is in effect until further notice.

Paintsville Utilities is advising all customers in the affected areas to boil their water before using it for drinking, making ice, cooking, washing dishes, brushing teeth or food preparation. Bring all water to a rolling boil, let it boil for three minutes, and let it cool before using. Boiling kills bacteria and other organisms in the water.

Due to measures required to complete a vital improvement project in the Hager Hill area, some Paintsville Utilities customers in Hager Hill and West Van Lear area will experience a water outage beginning around 9:00 am, Monday 2, 2023. The outage will last for several hours. The affected area will include:

\- Hager Hill from the intersection of RT. 1428 and RT. 321 to the intersection of RT. 1428 and RT. 302.

\- West Van Lear from the intersection of RT. 1428 and RT. 302 to the double set of tracks on RT. 302.

\- Apache Drive.

The affected area will be under a boil water advisory until further notice when service is restored.

PLANNED OUTAGE

WEDNESDAY  MAY 4, 2022

In order to complete connections on a project to improve service to some customers in the Blaine area of Lawrence County, water service will be interrupted to our customers along RT 32 from the bottom of Blaine Hill to the end of the line at the top of Blaine Hill.  The outage will begin at 9:00 am and service is expected to be restored around 3:00 pm.

The planned water outage affecting part of the RT 40 West area, scheduled for Monday, March 7, 2021, has been rescheduled for Tuesday, March 8, 2021. Weather permitting, the service interruption will begin about 9:00 am and we expect service to be restored by 3:00 pm. The areas affected are:



\- RT 40 W from Old Bridge Road to, but not including, Barnett’s Creek

\- Trimble Lane

\- RT 2275

\- Fishtrap Road

\- Blanton Subdivision

\- Paintsville Lake State Park



When service is restored, the affected areas will be placed under a precautionary boil water advisory.
